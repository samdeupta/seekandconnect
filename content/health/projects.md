## Rural Geriatric Care

_Started in March 2024_

After a decade of urban geriatric care, we've extended our mission to rural Bengal. We trained local women in geriatric care for 6 months, and equipped them with medical gadgets (pressure, blood sugar, weight, and temperature monitors) and with bicycles for easy commute. These dedicated caregivers now visit 500 seniors aged 65+ monthly, spending 30 minutes with each, monitoring vitals and offering emotional support. In just four months, this initiative has created strong bonds, with seniors eagerly anticipating visits. This project aims to enhance the mental and emotional well-being of our elderly community, ensuring they feel valued and cared for. We increased the coverage to 750 seniors in 2025, and a survey after a year of operation shows the intended benefits being realized.

## The Urban Geriatric Day-Care Project

_Started in 2023_

Seek & Connect collaborates with The Geriatric Association to provide a research based project that runs a day care for marginalised and impoverished elder women from the slums of Mukundapur, West Bengal. It is a self funded program and donations are raised through the Give portal. The funds are used to provide food, and to enhance the quality of life through vocational training, medical facilities, therapeutic intervention and entertainment activities.

This year (2025) we started working with them to channelize their creativity into marketable products. We are exploring whether we can create an opportunity for income generation.

## Empowering Rural Bengal with AI

_October 2024 to June 2025_

Over the past six months, we embarked on a journey to generate income for the women of rural Bengal. By connecting with a company specializing in training data for generative AI, we opened up new opportunities. This company needed help to reach deep into rural areas, and we leveraged our local connections to bridge that gap, motivating and training the participants.

Participants, equipped with a multilingual app, contribute to AI training data without the need for English proficiency. By working from their smartphones for a few hours for a task, they see a substantial supplemental income, earning about ₹500 per task, with a potential of ₹2,000 over six months.

This initiative is lighting up a path for AI in rural India, showing that technology can indeed empower and create new avenues for growth. We're excited for what the future holds!

## From Cash-Strapped to Thriving

_Started in 2025_

We met some incredible self-help groups (SHGs) in Basirhat who had cracked one big code: how to produce exactly what the market wants. By hiring their own marketing person, they secured orders first and then produced, a self-sustaining model. But they faced another hurdle: a lack of working capital to buy materials and scale up.

That's where our "Empowering Rural Bengal with AI" project stepped in. They channelized the income from there to boost their working capital and saw the benefits. We then worked with them to create a financial model, understanding exactly what they needed to unlock their full potential. The solution was an interest-free loan to boost their working capital.

The results, just in the first quarter, have been phenomenal:

- 3x increase in production
- Greater empowerment and stability for the SHGs

This is what happens when smart strategy meets community determination. We are so proud to be a part of their growth story!
