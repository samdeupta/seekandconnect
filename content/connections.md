This is an interactive platform for seniors and for the young at heart. Stimulating conversations on varied topics are held under the guidance of experts and specialists. These sessions not only open opportunities for sharing knowledge, learning and empowerment but also enable social interaction.

Covid-19 turned the world upside down, and social interactions ceased. The idea to promote community interactions took place virtually, and every week a topic was introduced and became a tool to engage in meaningful discussions. The topics covered Heritage & History, Hobbies & Passions, Culture & Entertainment, and Health & Wealth. Our senior participants loved these sessions as it relieved them of the boredom and monotony of their days.

Now that life has returned to something of the old normal, Seek & Connect – Connections continues to hold virtual and face to face events. These sessions give our participants the chance to interact and contribute to content-rich discussions and activities, and also have a fun filled evening.

## Outcomes

- 2020 to 2022: 60+ online sessions covering diverse topics.
- 2023: 3 cultural and social get-togethers.
- 2023: 1 river cruise with 50+ participants.
