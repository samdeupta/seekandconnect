Our education services provide support for remote schools and those at risk of dropping out, with no access to support or engagement. We help ensure all students have the opportunity to reach their educational goals and gain the skills they need to succeed.

Our Education Project has commenced in two areas of West Bengal: the Sunderbans, and Susunia in Bankura. We have two NGOs conducting the operations on the ground. All funds can be donated directly to the NGOs through Give.do.

The NGOs involved:

- Champa Mahila Samiti
- Kalyan Sangha
- Care Unlimited
