## Non-Formal Education

_Started in 2023_

We find that rural areas have a huge issue with quality education and accessibility for all. Often the schools are very far and this creates a reason for the students to disengage, stop going regularly and eventually drop off. Seek & Connect has started non-formal education centres in two remote areas of Sunderbans, West Bengal. They provide a teacher and study materials. The aim is to minimise drop out rates by empowering the students to keep up with the syllabus even if they have missed a few classes. The children also gain a mentor to guide them. Parents are involved to support this initiative, and those needing supplementary coaching are able to avail it at no extra cost.

We started 2 NFEs (Non-formal Education Centres) in January 2023, one in Jharkhali and another in Bali Island. Each centre supports around 25 primary school students with free tuition for two hours daily before their school starts. Two more NFEs were added on Bali Island in 2024. We are slowly institutionalizing structured learning through teaching aids like educational charts and worksheets for each grade.

We started 2 more such NFEs at Kalahazra, Sonakhali in January 2025. With 6 NFEs in operation covering 150 students, we have increased our focus on the quality of education. We coach and guide the teachers on a weekly basis to help them with their lesson plans, and enable them with appropriate TLM methods. We have conducted a teachers' workshop on TLM application. They are now equipped with additional teaching aids like flash cards, globes and maps, and a continuous supply of worksheets.

The impact of this effort is visible through the performance of the kids. They are being amply appreciated in their schools. To facilitate the overall grooming of the children, we have started organizing annual sports for them from 2025.

## Hands on Science

_Started in 2022_

Seek & Connect holds practical science sessions in schools in the Sunderbans, West Bengal. These science experiments are fun and interactive, and use basic materials. Students learn science through experiments and practical knowledge, by engaging and stimulating their minds in looking, observing, doing, interacting and then drawing a conclusion. The goal is to infuse a genuine interest and inquisitiveness about science and how it affects our lives.

We have been conducting day-long science workshops in 5 remote and under-privileged schools.

## Interactive Learning

_Started in 2023_

Students are given hand held devices such as tablets loaded with learning apps and interactive lessons to make learning fun. These devices are shared and not only have access to a variety of uses, they also teach the students to be responsible and technologically savvy.

The initial pilot showed very promising results. The kids have developed their number skills and English vocabulary faster than in the normal mode. This result has prompted us to spread this to two locations in the Sunderbans, one site in Susunia, and one under-privileged school in Bijoygarh, Kolkata.

## Inter Generational Education

_Started in 2018_

Senior citizens visit under-privileged schools to tutor in English and Mathematics and share their life experiences and stories, enriching the students' lives. In return the seniors feel appreciated and get a good feeling to give back to society. This was operational in one site in Kolkata at Bijoygarh. However, post pandemic we could not organize adequate senior volunteers, and the model is operational through one senior only.
