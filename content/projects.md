Seek & Connect is a community welfare group who provide a wide range of services to benefit our wider community, especially under-privileged, marginalised and rural communities.

We also bring much needed support and an interactive platform for our senior citizens.

We offer educational programs, environmental work and initiatives to support community welfare.

## Education

Our education services provide support for remote schools and those at risk of dropping out, with no access to support or engagement. We help ensure all students have the opportunity to reach their educational goals and gain the skills they need to succeed.

[Read more](/education/)

## Environment

Our environment community help service is dedicated to fighting climate change and protecting our environment. We are committed to reducing erosion through tree plantation and reforestation initiatives. We are also actively involved in promoting green practices and educating the public about the importance of preserving our environment for future generations.

[Read more](/environment/)

## Connection

This is an interactive platform for seniors and for the young at heart. Stimulating conversations on varied topics are held under the guidance of experts and specialists. These sessions not only open opportunities for sharing knowledge, learning and empowerment but also enable social interaction.

[Read more](/connections/)

## Community

We believe in the power of community connections. Our mission is to bring together people from all walks of life, foster new relationships, and promote unity. We are dedicated to empowering women, supporting senior projects and helping everyone in our community get the resources they need to thrive. We believe in the incredible power of people coming together for a common goal.
